Craft notes

Rooted in Mewar, cut for now

Rajasthan has dyed, tied and embroidered cloth for generations. We keep those techniques and change the cuts, fabrics and fits to suit how you dress today.

Bandhani

Cloth is tied into small knots before it is dyed. When the knots open, the dots appear.

Leheriya

Cloth is rolled and tied, then dyed, to make diagonal waves. It is linked with the monsoon festivals.

Gota patti

Gold-coloured ribbon is cut, folded and stitched onto the fabric by hand.

Zardozi

Metal thread and beads are stitched to lift the pattern off the cloth.
